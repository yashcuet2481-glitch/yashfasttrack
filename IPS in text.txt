              <h2>Rahul Gandhi Demands Justice in IPS Officer Y Puran Kumar’s Death</h2>
              <p><strong>Written By:</strong> Hindustan Times Staff | <strong>Published:</strong> October 14, 2025 – 12:56 PM IST</p>
              <hr>
              <img src="rahuljpg.jpg" alt="Rahul Gandhi meets IPS officer's family" width="100%" style="max-width:700px; border-radius:8px; margin-bottom:20px; box-shadow:0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.1); border:1px solid #ccc;">
              <p>Congress leader Rahul Gandhi on Tuesday met the family of IPS officer Y Puran Kumar in Chandigarh, days after the officer allegedly died by suicide at his residence in Haryana. The incident has triggered widespread outrage, drawing attention to alleged caste-based discrimination within the police and bureaucratic systems.</p>
              <p>Following the meeting, Gandhi urged Haryana Chief Minister Nayab Singh Saini and Prime Minister Narendra Modi to take immediate action. He stated that the tragedy sends a damaging message to Dalit communities — that even success does not shield them from systemic oppression. “This is not just about the dignity of one family, but about the dignity of all Dalits,” he said.</p>
              <p>Gandhi also criticized the Haryana government for failing to uphold its promise of a transparent investigation. He claimed that the late officer’s daughters are under immense pressure and called on authorities to honor their commitment and allow the funeral to proceed respectfully.</p>
              <p><strong>📄 Allegations and Legal Demands</strong></p>
              <p>Y Puran Kumar passed away on October 7, reportedly from a self-inflicted gunshot wound. According to his wife, IAS officer Amneet Puran Kumar, he left behind an eight-page note naming senior officials — including Haryana Director General of Police Shatrujeet Kapur and Rohtak Superintendent of Police Narendra Bijarniya — and accusing them of caste-based harassment, mental torture, and public humiliation.</p>
              <p>Amneet has filed a formal complaint seeking an FIR against the named officials and demanded that the case be investigated under appropriate sections of the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act. The postmortem of Y Puran Kumar remains pending, as the family has withheld consent until their demands are addressed.</p>
