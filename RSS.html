RSS Centenary: The Sangh Playbook for Trumping the Odds

Written By: Hindustan Times Opinion Desk | Published: October 18, 2025 – 9:00 AM IST

As the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) approaches its centenary, political analysts are closely watching how the organization adapts its strategies to remain relevant in a rapidly changing India. From grassroots mobilization to ideological recalibration, the Sangh has demonstrated resilience and tactical evolution.

Recent moves suggest a shift toward broader social engagement, including outreach to marginalized communities and increased emphasis on education and entrepreneurship. While critics argue that the RSS remains rigid in its core beliefs, supporters highlight its ability to influence policy without direct political participation.

The centenary celebrations are expected to showcase the Sangh’s historical legacy and future roadmap. Key speeches and publications will likely reinforce its cultural narrative while addressing contemporary challenges such as digital mobilization and youth engagement.

Whether one agrees with its ideology or not, the RSS’s organizational discipline and long-term vision offer lessons in political persistence. As India’s political landscape continues to fragment, the Sangh’s centenary may mark not just a milestone, but a strategic pivot.
