Maithili Thakur Joins BJP Ahead of Bihar Polls

Written By: Rishabh Sinha | Published: October 14, 2025 – 6:45 PM IST

Renowned folk and devotional singer Maithili Thakur officially joined the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) on Tuesday during the Milan Samaroh event in Patna, just ahead of the 2025 Bihar Assembly elections. Expressing admiration for Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Bihar Chief Minister Nitish Kumar, Thakur said their leadership inspired her to contribute to society through their vision.

“I’ve always been moved by PM Modi’s speeches and Nitish Kumar’s work. Their dedication to public service encouraged me to support their mission,” she stated.

Thakur emphasized that her entry into politics stems from a desire to serve rather than personal ambition. “Joining a party doesn’t make one a politician. I’m here to spread their ideology and serve the people,” she said.

Identifying herself as the “daughter of Mithila,” Thakur reaffirmed her deep connection to her roots. “My soul belongs to Mithila,” she added.

Regarding her role within the BJP, Thakur said she would follow the party’s direction. “Whatever responsibility the party gives me, I’m ready to take it on,” she affirmed.

Her induction followed a meeting with BJP national general secretary Vinod Tawde and Union Minister Nityanand Rai. Thakur expressed openness to contesting the upcoming elections if nominated, saying, “It would be a new journey, and I’m prepared for it.”

She mentioned her strong ties to both Madhubani and Darbhanga districts, noting, “My paternal grandmother’s village is in Madhubani, and my maternal grandmother’s is in Darbhanga. I’m loved in both places, but the final decision rests with the party.”

Sources within the BJP suggest she may be considered for the Benipatti or Alinagar Assembly seat, though no official announcement has been made.

Thakur rose to fame as the runner-up in the 2017 reality show ‘The Rising Star’ and has since built a massive following on social media platforms like Instagram and YouTube. She concluded, “I’ve been serving Bihar through music. If politics offers another path to serve, I’ll embrace it wholeheartedly.”
