Two Terrorists Neutralized in Kupwara, J&K; Search Operations Intensify

Indian Army launches search operations after successful counter-infiltration in Machhal Sector.

On the evening of October 13, 2025, Indian Army personnel successfully eliminated two terrorists in the Machhal Sector of Kupwara district, Jammu and Kashmir. The operation was initiated following credible intelligence inputs from the Jammu and Kashmir Police (JKP), supported by other agencies, indicating a possible infiltration attempt across the Line of Control (LoC).

According to the Army’s official statement, troops observed suspicious movement and issued a challenge. In response, the terrorists opened indiscriminate fire, prompting a swift and effective counteraction by the forces. Both terrorists were neutralized during the exchange, and a substantial cache of weapons and equipment was recovered from the site.

Following the encounter, comprehensive search operations have been launched across the region to ensure no further threats remain.

🚨 High Alert Issued Ahead of Winter: Amit Shah’s Directive

In light of the seasonal shift and the increased risk of infiltration during snowfall, Union Home Minister Amit Shah has directed security forces to maintain heightened vigilance along the LoC and International Border. The concern is that adverse weather conditions may be exploited by terrorist groups to breach Indian territory.

This directive was issued during a high-level security review meeting held in New Delhi. The meeting was attended by key officials including Jammu and Kashmir Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha, the Union Home Secretary, Director of Intelligence Bureau, Chief of Army Staff, Chief Secretary and Director General of Police of Jammu and Kashmir, as well as senior representatives from the Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and other government bodies.

The government reaffirmed its commitment to national security and emphasized proactive measures to counter any infiltration attempts during the winter months.
