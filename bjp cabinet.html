BJP Releases Third List of Candidates for Bihar Assembly Elections

Written By: CNBC TV18 Staff | Published: October 16, 2025 – 12:24 AM IST

The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) has released its third list of candidates for the upcoming Bihar Assembly elections. The announcement includes several new faces and strategic placements aimed at consolidating support in key constituencies.

Party leaders stated that the selection process focused on grassroots feedback, performance metrics, and caste balance. The BJP is expected to contest the elections in alliance with JD(U), with seat-sharing talks reportedly in the final stages.

With the release of this list, the party has now declared candidates for over 80% of the seats it plans to contest. The remaining names are likely to be announced after internal consultations and feedback from local units.
